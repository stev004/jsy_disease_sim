## The eleven decisions

**1. Design study; outcomes authorize nothing beyond input to a later Steven decision — AMEND (A1).** Correct in substance. One sentence leaves a door ajar: "a decision may rely on it only after a separately owner-ruled, fresh-seed confirmatory campaign" could be read as a two-step path to real-data fitting. A confirmatory campaign would itself be synthetic; neither it nor this study can reopen G34 or satisfy the V1.3 plan's Phase-0 gate. A1 says so in §1 and §7.4. With A1, no reading of any outcome licenses real-data fitting.

**2. P0-2, P0-3 and G29 excluded — ACCEPT.** The study is not a gate and does not need all-arms evidence; both arms passed twice; the question is recoverability of the offset jointly with beta and detection. Excluding them removes no protection and adds no surface for retuning.

**3. Ten targets per arm, fractional thresholds in integer arithmetic — ACCEPT (reading fixed by A8).** The argument is grid-independent and result-independent: the Bernoulli operating characteristic is a property of the threshold fraction alone. Every fraction (4/5, 3/5, ≤ 1/5) is preserved; the change is a tightening at low p with no gain at high p, so it cannot be read as making a PASS easier. It is direction-neutral in the Phase-0b sense (the same choice would be made whatever Phase 0b had selected). The only consequence the draft gets wrong is comparability: a ten-target fresh-seed baseline is not a replication of the five-target Phase-0b design, so a B PASS must not be read as "the Phase-0b failure did not replicate" (A8). Cost (+5 builds, +5 latent, +5 transforms per campaign) is trivial.

**4. Arm (i): R = 12 as four batches of three, batch A = B's candidates, nested 3/6/12, descriptives — AMEND (A2).** R = 12 is accepted as the level. Its structural justification stands on its own: it is the smallest multiple of B's replicate count that halves the Monte-Carlo SD of every candidate mean (R = 4·3), it keeps every batch Phase-0b-shaped so the batches are independent draws of the baseline objective at identical tuples, and R = 48 (the next doubling of precision) is a 16× cost. Two sentences must go: "the smallest step that would plausibly turn a step comparable to its scatter into one twice its scatter" imports the Phase-0b observed scatter into the justification, and "four draws are the minimum that yield a range and a median" is false (two give a range, three a median). A2 replaces the bullet. The nesting order A ⊂ A∪B′ ⊂ all is declared in advance and is fine. The descriptives are correctly labelled as stochastic replicate variation.

**5. Arm (ii): probe design and H rule; stop rule; prefix-identity consequence; mechanical fill — AMEND (A3, A4) with conditions.** The probe is legitimate under four conditions, which A4 and the Conditions section make binding: the rule is frozen by this ruling's commit before any probe simulation; probe seeds are disjoint from every study set and retired afterwards; the probe computes nothing but p_s, e_s, the prefix-identity result and runtimes (no loss, no profile, no candidate cell); its record is hashed and filed before the frozen text. Under those conditions the probe reveals only the timing shape of a declared synthetic truth, which is exactly the quantity the factor is defined by, and nothing about the candidate surface. On the rule itself: the 10% decline mark is a declared synthetic assumption and is acceptable as such. The margin is not adequately argued as written: the draft calls it a "one-week margin" and writes +8. A3 fixes the inconsistency by deriving 8 from declared fixed parameters only (infection-to-report lag 2 + 0 + 2 = 4 days under the frozen observation model, plus the 4-day observation tail), which is a mechanical consequence of the Phase-0b scenario, not a new number. The multiple-of-7 rounding has no observation rationale (weekday factors are all 1.0) and is restated as a rounding convention; the 35 floor and 91 cap are accepted as declared bounds. The stop rule is accepted. The prefix-identity labelling consequence is accepted; the draft is honest that Starsim RNG identity is unverified. Mechanical fill is accepted. A truth-cell probe conditions H on truth dynamics; low-beta candidate cells may not peak within H, and that is a property of those candidates, scored as such.

**6. Arm (iii) synthetic serosurvey assumptions — AMEND (A5, A6, A7).** Day 29: accepted as the last latent day (any later date is a second factor). Zero lag and a perfect assay: accepted as the idealization of the estimand, correctly labelled; inventing a lag or sensitivity would violate the no-invented-parameters rule. One sentence over-reads: "A FAIL here is therefore decisive against this anchor at this n and timing." A FAIL says the anchor at this n, this date, one survey and this weight did not produce recovery; it says nothing about larger n, multiple dates or other weights. A6 replaces it. n = 400: the criterion is tied to the declared grid (one symptomatic-detection grid step at truth) and to an explicit 25% floor; the floor is the binding assumption and is declared as one; it is conservative in the direction that makes n larger, not smaller, so it cannot be read as tuned to reduce cost. A7 requires the realized ever-infected fraction at d to be reported per target so an auditor can see whether the criterion's premise held (descriptive, never gating). Equal per-datum weight 1/(2T): accepted. Both terms are variance-stabilized to ≈ 1/4, so equal weight is approximately inverse-variance weighting and the anchor's influence enters through n alone; there is no tunable multiplier. The serosurvey term is constant across detection cells for a fixed (process seed, beta, offset), which is the mechanism by which it separates scale from detection; that is correct and the draft's reuse count (75) follows. Seed-only SRS: accepted, but the hash input must be byte-specified for reproducibility (A5). The estimand is unique identities, never episodes, with the census cross-check in U4 against `ever_infected_fraction`, which the code confirms is identity-based.

**7. Seed table and pre-freeze replacement rule; fallback-built populations count as builds — ACCEPT (A10 adds visibility).** The rule is deterministic, outcome-blind, applied once before any study simulation, and records what was excluded and why. Fallback-built populations are kept: the fallback is reviewed, merged code (G36); excluding them would select the study population on a structural property the product does not select on, and target subsetting is already forbidden. The G36 point-mass caveat is disclosed in §15. A10 makes each target's and candidate's fallback status a column in the recovery CSV and a per-arm descriptive count, so the caveat is visible in the bundle and not only in prose.

**8. Exact workload caps; wall time and bundle size as disclosures — ACCEPT.** Every count recomputed and correct. Dry-run must additionally enforce the (H + 4)-date calendar for arm (ii) (already implied by "exactly the frozen H"). Host free-space check per the 2026-09-02 lesson is present.

**9. Standalone harness; `phase0_campaign.py` and protected modules unchanged; stop on conflict — ACCEPT.** The cited hard-codes exist at the cited lines. Importing pure functions and proving bit-for-bit equality at R = 3, T = 34 is the right independent source of truth.

**10. Three primary comparisons, readings table, multiplicity — AMEND (A1, A8, A9).** The three comparisons and the exclusions are accepted; they close the forking paths. Multiplicity handling (no threshold adjustment; every PASS is screening evidence; confirmatory requires Steven and a new ruling) is accepted because any adjustment would require a number not justified independently. Three fixes: the PASS/PASS reading over-reads (A8); the state-file vocabulary conflates descriptive readings with gate verdicts and must carry PASS/FAIL per arm with VOID as a campaign status per Phase-0b §12.3 (A9); and the confirmatory-campaign sentence (A1).

## Conditions on the pre-freeze actions

1. **Order.** Ruling commit (A11) → build pre-check → horizon probe → frozen text. Every step is a trail row through `fm.sh log`; the probe row cites the ruling commit SHA and SHA-256.
2. **Build pre-check** records, per process seed: build outcome, exception class if any, `private_assignment_residual_fallback` and `destination_reassignment_fallback` presence, and M2/M3/M4 logical hashes. Replacement follows §8 verbatim; the record lists every exclusion and its replacement. Reserve exhaustion returns the draft to the owner, not a widened pool.
3. **Probe** runs only the truth cell on seeds `81001–81003` with observation seeds `91001–91003`, at 30 and 91 days, and records only p_s, e_s, H, the prefix-identity result (identity/not identity, with the two hash pairs compared), and `runtime_seconds` per call. No loss, profile, candidate cell or alternative H is computed or written. If the stop rule fires, the record says so and the draft returns NEEDS OWNER INPUT; H is never chosen by hand.
4. **Host disk** free space ≥ 5 GB checked and logged before the probe (2026-09-02 lesson).
5. **Frozen text** contains the final seeds, exclusions, hashes, H, the prefix-identity result and the A1–A11 text verbatim; its SHA-256 is fixed in the config and in an independent module constant (Phase-0b §12.5 pattern). Any later inconsistency is a stop resolved only by a filed clarification ruling that changes no grid, threshold, seed, objective, level or predicate.
6. **Vocabulary** from this point: "Phase 0: FAIL (2026-09-24); Phase 0b: FAIL (2026-09-26); G35-B: not run" in every state file until a campaign completes (A9). G34 stays STOPPED throughout.
