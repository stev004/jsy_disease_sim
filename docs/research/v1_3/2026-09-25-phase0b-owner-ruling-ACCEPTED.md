## The six decisions

**1. Post-Phase-0 design disclosed; Phase-0 FAIL stands — ACCEPT.**
The Phase-0 exit verdict of 2026-09-24 is FAIL and remains FAIL. No Phase-0b outcome alters it, and no state file, plan or report may ever describe Phase 0 as having passed. The frozen document must say, in its status line and in the bundle's lineage block, that Phase 0b was designed after Phase-0 results were observed. The justification for a redesign at all is that the flaw is visible from the frozen Phase-0 text without any result: with a three-point grid centred on truth and a one-step tolerance, every point on every grid lay within tolerance, so predicate 5 could not fail and predicate 8 (never designed as the recovery threshold) was carrying the whole recovery burden. That is a design defect, not an unlucky draw, and a specification with a vacuous recovery predicate should not be the gate that unlocks real-data fitting.

**2. Five-point grids at half spacing; tolerance and bias limits halved — ACCEPT.**
Every new number is mechanically derived: each spacing is half the frozen Phase-0 spacing, tolerance remains "one grid step" and bias limit remains "half a grid step" (both rules unchanged from Phase 0), the truths are unchanged and stay at grid centre. Nothing is chosen. The asymmetry between the symptomatic step (0.125) and the asymptomatic step (0.075) is inherited from the frozen Phase-0 spacings (0.25 and 0.15), not introduced; all grid points are finite decimals inside [0, 1] and inside the 30-day horizon, so the exact-decimal arithmetic already required by the 2026-09-22 lesson applies without modification. This is a tightening in absolute terms (recovery within half the old step) and direction-neutral: the observed Phase-0 pattern (two targets at the outer Phase-0 values) would fail Phase 0b under predicates 5 and 8 alike, and the same fix would have been correct had the misses been one-sided or absent. I also rule out the alternatives considered: keeping three points and setting tolerance to zero contradicts the declared Phase-0 intent of tolerating one stochastic displacement; seven points at half spacing is infeasible (offset −1 and symptomatic probability 1.125 are not valid). The finer grid raises the chance that the unchanged 5% profile-gap rule declares a dimension non-identified; an identification failure so caused is a legitimate Phase-0b FAIL and the 5% threshold is not revisited by anyone.

**3. Predicate 8 retained at 1/5 with its redundancy — ACCEPT, with the redundancy made explicit and the predicate computed independently (amendment).**
Decided: keep predicate 8 verbatim; do not re-specify it. Reasoning: on the new grid a boundary is two steps from truth, hence outside tolerance, hence predicate 5 (at most one target outside tolerance per dimension) implies predicate 8 (at most one boundary per dimension). I considered giving it independent work — a joint-corner check ("no target at the boundary in two or more dimensions"), a pooled cap across all 20 selections, or a wider grid — and reject all three: a wider grid is infeasible (above), and a new predicate adds post-result design surface that is not needed to correct the flaw and would weaken the strongest anti-retuning argument this design has, namely that exactly one quantity changed. Keeping the failing predicate byte-identical (same outermost values 0/4, 0.04/0.12, 0.50/1.00, 0.10/0.40; same 1/5) means Phase 0b cannot be read as having removed or softened the predicate that failed. Conditions: the frozen text states the implication plainly; the harness computes predicate 8 by its own code path (never derived from predicate 5) and reports the per-dimension boundary count; the implementation reviewer checks that the check has an independent source of truth per the 2026-09-23 lesson. The same principle governs the P0-2A "offset = 4" clause (decision 4).

**4. Fresh seed sets; rerun P0-2A, P0-2B and P0-3 in one campaign under unchanged G29 — ACCEPT; keep the offset-4 clause verbatim (amendment makes the redundancy visible).**
Seeds 62001–62005 / 72001–72005 / 63001–63003 / 73001–73003 are disjoint from every Phase-0 set and from each other; they are reproducibility identifiers, not parameters. Rerun is required, not optional: P0-2A's detection clauses reference the Phase-0b tolerances and the 625-cell grid, so Phase-0's P0-2A evidence is a different test; P0-3 is unchanged in design but the one-campaign, one-bundle, one-seed-ledger, fresh-auditor contract (and the Phase-0 rule that no arm may react to another arm's results) requires contemporaneous evidence, and its cost is trivial (27 latent calls). Phase-0's P0-2/P0-3 PASS results enter the lineage block as historical evidence only. On the offset-4 clause: it is implied by the outside-tolerance clause on this grid exactly as predicate 8 is implied by predicate 5, and for consistency it is kept verbatim; each of the three P0-2A clauses is evaluated by its own code path and its per-target TRUE/FALSE/UNKNOWN value written to the CSV, so an auditor sees the redundancy rather than assuming it. G29 applies unchanged; the clause is a selected-candidate clause and takes UNKNOWN under a tied wrong-model minimum, as before.

**5. Budget 1,334 cells / 107 latent calls / 4,007 transforms / 8 builds, fail-closed — ACCEPT (amendment adds measured disclosures).**
Arithmetic verified independently: P0-1 latent = 5 truth + 3 × 5 × 5 = 80 (latent runs do not depend on detection probabilities), transforms 5 + 625 × 3 = 1,880; P0-2A 0 / 1,875; P0-2B 0 / 225; P0-3 27 / 27; totals 107 and 4,007; builds 5 + 3 = 8. The expensive quantity (latent 3,000-resident 30-day CI runs) rises 59 → 107, about 1.8×; the 6.7× rise is in cheap observation transforms. Acceptable at CI scale under the existing execution authorization path (section 7 step 6); no 180-day wave or 30-replicate ensemble is touched. Wall time and bundle size are recorded as measured disclosures, not caps, and cannot justify a shortcut.

**6. Separate frozen Phase-0b profile in the existing standalone harness; Phase-0 behaviour preserved; protected modules untouched — ACCEPT.**
Two exact, separately frozen declarations selected by campaign ID; the Phase-0 profile must still dry-run to 198 / 59 / 599 / 8 and validate against its own digest, proven by a focused regression. The Phase-0b digest lives in the config and in an independent module constant (never derived from the supplied config). No write through `CalibrationArtifactManifest` / `write_calibration_artifact`; any protected-contract conflict is a stop condition, not a workaround.

## Why this is not result-driven retuning

Phase 0b changes exactly one design quantity, grid resolution, and the change is dictated by a flaw that can be read off the frozen Phase-0 text with no results in hand: a one-step tolerance on a three-point grid centred on truth makes the recovery predicate vacuous and pushes every tolerated displacement onto a grid boundary. The tolerance and bias rules ("one step", "half a step") are unchanged and merely re-evaluated on the halved spacing, so every absolute limit tightens; the truths, objective, tie rule, 5% profile-gap rule, identification rule, per-dimension and joint hit thresholds, bias-limit rule, boundary limit, P0-2 detection clauses and thresholds, P0-3 design and the G29 ruling are all retained verbatim. The predicate on which Phase 0 failed is kept byte-identical in meaning and still fails the observed Phase-0 pattern; the same correction would have been made whether the observed misses had been two-sided, one-sided or absent. Fresh, disjoint seeds mean no observed realization is reused, and the Phase-0 FAIL is preserved as lineage in every output. Nothing in this design makes a Phase-0b PASS easier to obtain than a Phase-0 PASS would have been; it makes it harder, and it makes a FAIL, should one occur, a finding the gate can express.
