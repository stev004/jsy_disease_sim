Number of teachers currently employed in Jersey

Number of teachers currently employed in Jersey

Produced by Freedom of Information office
Authored by People Services and published on 04 August 2025.
Prepared internally, no external costs.

​Request ​7320​66431​

As of the most recent data, please provide the following information ;-

1. The number of teachers (FTE). This to include school teachers, heads, deputy heads and lecturers

2. The number of other staff (FTE). This to include civil servants, manual workers, technical assistants, youth service staff, Highlands College managers, residential and childcare officers.

3. The number of teachers' assistants. If the number is included under 3. above, also show the number separately.

Response

In response to questions 1, 2 and 3 above, and as at 16 July 2025, the full time equivalent (FTE) headcount within the CYPES department was as shown in the below table.
